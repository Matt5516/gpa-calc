# TermMath

Free GPA calculator. Enter your grades and credits to get your semester GPA as you type, then plan the GPA you need next term or the score you need on a final.

**Live site: [https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [GPA calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/gpa-calculator/): Grades and credits in, your semester GPA out. Add your current GPA for a cumulative GPA and see the one grade that moves it most.
- [Cumulative GPA calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/cumulative-gpa-calculator/): Prior GPA and credits plus this term, or several terms, to one cumulative GPA.
- [Weighted GPA calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/weighted-gpa-calculator/): High school GPA: weighted and unweighted side by side, with editable honors, AP, IB and dual enrollment bumps.
- [Raise your GPA (target planner)](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/raise-gpa-calculator/): What average you need over your next credits to hit a target GPA, and how many credits of A’s it takes.
- [Final grade calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/final-grade-calculator/): What you need on the final to get the grade you want, plus the best and worst case. Or work out your course grade after the final.
- [Grade converter (percent, letter, GPA)](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/grade-converter/): Percent to letter to GPA points, and back, with the full scale and your row highlighted.
- [GPA scale converter](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/gpa-scale-converter/): Convert a GPA between 4.0, 4.3, 5.0 and 100-point scales, with the straight-proportion math shown.
- [Class grade calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/gpa-calc/grade-calculator/): Weighted categories (homework, tests, final) to your current grade, and what you need on what is left.

## Data sources
GPA follows the standard formula: quality points = grade points × credits, and GPA = total quality points ÷ GPA credits. The default grade points (A 4.0, A− 3.7, B+ 3.3 and so on) and the percent bands (a common 10-point scale) are a typical US scale, and every tool lets you edit them. The score needed on a final is (target − grade now × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight, and the course grade after it is grade now × (1 − weight) + final score × weight. Schools set their own scales, weights and retake rules, and your registrar's number is the official one.
