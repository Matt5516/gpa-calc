Raise your GPA from 3.1 to 3.4

How many credits, at which averages.

From a 3.1 over 60 credits, you need 30 credits of straight A’s, 60 credits at a 3.7 average or 180 credits at a 3.5 average to reach 3.4. The more credits you already have, the longer it takes.

FAQ

How many credits of A’s do I need to raise a 3.1 to a 3.4?

From 60 credits, about 30 credits of straight A’s. From 30 credits it is 15 and from 90 credits 45.

What average do I need over 30 credits to go from 3.1 to 3.4 at 60 credits?

A 4.00 average over those 30 credits.

Can I raise 3.1 to 3.4 in one semester?

With 60 credits and 15 more at a perfect 4.0 you reach 3.28; that falls short of 3.4, so it takes more than one semester.

Why does raising a GPA take longer with more credits?

Because each new credit is a smaller share of the total. Going from 3.1 to 3.4 at straight A’s takes 8 credits from 15 and 45 from 90.
