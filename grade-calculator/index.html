Class grade calculator

Categories and weights in. Your grade and what’s left out.

The math: Current grade = the weighted average of the categories you have scores for: sum of (weight × score) ÷ the weights entered. Leave a score empty and it is treated as work still to come; the score you need on it uses the final grade formula.

The short answer

With homework (20%) at 94, quizzes (10%) at 88 and a midterm (30%) at 81, your grade so far is 86.5%. To finish with a 90% you need 95.3% on the final exam (the last 40%).

Worked example

CategoryWeightScoreWeight × score
Homework20%941,880
Quizzes10%88880
Midterm30%812,430
Final exam40%not yet—
So far60%5,190 ÷ 60 = 86.5%

For a 90% overall, the last 40% has to average (90 − 86.5 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = 95.3%. Weights do not have to add up to 100; the calculator uses their total.

Syllabi sometimes drop the lowest score or round up. If yours does, ask your teacher how it works.

FAQ

How do I calculate my grade with weighted categories?

Multiply each category’s weight by your score, add them, and divide by the weights you have scores for. Here (20 × 94 + 10 × 88 + 30 × 81) ÷ 60 = 86.5%.

What do I need on the rest to reach my target?

It is the final grade formula with the remaining weight: (target − grade so far × weight done) ÷ weight left. A 90% target needs 95.3% on the last 40% here.

Do the weights need to add up to 100?

No. The calculator divides by the total of the weights you enter.

What if a category has several scores?

Average them first (or add the points earned and the points possible) and enter the category’s percent.
