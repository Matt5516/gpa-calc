The math: Cumulative GPA = (prior GPA × prior credits + this term’s quality points) ÷ (prior credits + this term’s credits). It weights every credit the same, whichever term it was in.

The short answer

A 3.2 GPA over 45 credits is 144.0 quality points. Add this term’s 40.9 quality points over 12 credits and you have 184.9 ÷ 57 = 3.24. Your cumulative GPA is 3.24.

FAQ

How do I calculate cumulative GPA?

Add up all quality points (GPA × credits for each term) and divide by all credits. With 3.2 over 45 credits and a 3.41 term over 12 credits: (144.0 + 40.9) ÷ 57 = 3.24.

Can I average my semester GPAs?

Only if every term has the same number of credits. Otherwise weight each term by its credits, which is what the multiple terms mode does.

Which credits count in the prior total?

The GPA credits (also called GPA hours or credits attempted for GPA). Pass/fail and transfer credits that do not enter your GPA are left out. Ask your registrar if you are unsure.

What if I do not know my prior credits?

Your transcript or student portal lists GPA credits next to your cumulative GPA. Without them the new GPA cannot be worked out.
